# Product and insights (Weather desk)

| Role in RBAC | Demo sign-in | Home screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lead or agent` | `meera.iyer@opsdesk.demo` | `/tower/insights` |

**What they need:** recurring themes, with examples, turned into backlog items.

## A day in OpsDesk

1. Opens **Weather Radar**: an eight-week heat map of themes.
2. Spots a theme flagged **emerging** (a real spike, not a trend).
3. Reads the sample tickets behind it.
4. Attaches a Jira or roadmap link to the theme so ops sees it is being fixed.

## Features they use

- Weather Radar heat map and week-over-week change
- Emerging flag with z-score
- Sample tickets and agency count
- Editable product link per theme

## Tip

Themes with many agencies but few tickets each often point at a platform issue rather than one supplier.
