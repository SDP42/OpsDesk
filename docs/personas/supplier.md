# Supplier Partner (our addition)

| Role in RBAC | Demo sign-in | Home screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `supplier` | `partner.atlas@opsdesk.demo` | `/partner` |

**What they need:** to answer TBO quickly without long email chains.

## A day in OpsDesk

1. Signs in to the partner portal and sees only their own channels.
2. Opens a Frequency that TBO started about a booking.
3. Replies with a quick reply ("Confirmed, HCN to follow") or free text.
4. The reply lands on the ticket timeline live for the controller.
5. Tracks their own reply speed on the portal.

## Features they use

- Partner channels (scoped by RBAC)
- Quick replies
- Response-time stats

## Tip

Quick replies keep answers consistent and count toward the supplier scorecard TBO sees.
