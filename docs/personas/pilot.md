# Pilot (agency requester)

| Role in RBAC | Demo sign-in | Home screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `requester` | `pilot.saf@opsdesk.demo` | `/pilot` |

**What they need:** status of their own flights, and never having to explain the same booking twice.

## A day in OpsDesk

1. Types what happened and the TBOH booking ID in **File a flight**.
2. OpsDesk fills the hotel, guest, check-in and LCD from the booking and shows earlier cases on it (*continue existing case*).
3. Intent auto-suggest shows the top three problem types with confidence.
4. The flight appears on the **departures board** with live status and ETA.
5. Later, they ask Tower Radio "where is my refund?" instead of calling.

## Features they use

- Departures board with live status
- TBOH auto-fill and earlier-case prompt
- Intent auto-suggest
- Replies from the controller (internal notes stay hidden)
- Tower Radio voice questions

## Tip

Use **Continue existing case** when the booking already has an open flight: the controller keeps the whole story in one place.
