# Airport Director (ops lead)

| Role in RBAC | Demo sign-in | Home screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lead` | `meera.iyer@opsdesk.demo` | `/tower, /tower/simulate, /tower/data` |

**What they need:** queue health, SLA risk, routing policy and reporting without limits.

## A day in OpsDesk

1. Starts on the **Tower** overview: radar, open flights, SLA risk and health tiles.
2. Runs the **routing simulator** on yesterday's flights and applies the winning preset.
3. Syncs the **Crew Roster** from Darwinbox and re-routes flights owned by people on leave.
4. Reviews **Ghost Flights** and sends pre-emptive messages.
5. Streams an uncapped export from the **Black Box**.

## Features they use

- Overview radar and stats
- Routing presets and simulator
- Crew Roster (HRMS) and rebalance
- Ghost Flights, supplier scorecard
- Black Box export, archive run, Chaos Mode for demos

## Tip

Run the simulator before changing the preset: it replays real flights, so you see the breach trade-off in numbers.
