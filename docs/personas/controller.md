# Controller (ops agent)

| Role in RBAC | Demo sign-in | Home screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `agent` | `priya.nair@opsdesk.demo` | `/tower, /tower/queue` |

**What they need:** history, links, intent, correct routing and clean side conversations.

## A day in OpsDesk

1. Opens **My queue**, sorted by fuel left (time to the guest's check-in).
2. Opens a flight: linked flights, agency turbulence, wheels-up clock and breach forecast on one screen.
3. Reads **Why this route** and the Co-pilot Brief.
4. Opens a **Frequency** to loop in the supplier with locked recipients.
5. Replies to the Pilot, or escalates with a reason.

## Features they use

- Queue and triage views
- Flight detail with linked history and Guardian banner
- Vectoring waterfall and runners-up
- Co-pilot Brief, Breach Forecast, Frustration Radar
- Frequencies (internal vs external) and HandoffDiff on transfer
- Constellation and Ask the Tower (⌘K)

## Tip

Check the Breach Forecast before escalating: if calling the supplier drops the odds more, do that first.
